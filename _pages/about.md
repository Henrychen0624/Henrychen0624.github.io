Hello, welcome to my personal website. My name is Zhihan Chen and I am a graduate student in Financial Engineering at Cornell University with a strong foundation in applied mathematics and a passion for quantitative research and systematic trading strategies. I earned my bachelor’s degree from UCLA, where I honed my skills in mathematical modeling, data analysis, and machine learning. Before I came to the United States, I lived in China for 20 years. My hometown is in Hangzhou, a really beautiful city with nice people, prosperous city centers, and beautiful landscapes. I don't know where I will go after graduation, but Hangzhou would definitely be one of my top choices. In my kindergarten life, I lived in Yongkang, Jinhua, another beautiful city in China. It might not be as prosperous as Hangzhou but since I was born there, Yongkang felt more warm for me:)
